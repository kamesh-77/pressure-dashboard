Who holds up when the match is on the line?

788 IPL and T20I matches from 2022 to 2026, 188,151 deliveries. A ball counts as pressure when it can swing the win probability at least 1.5 times as much as an average ball. Players are rated on what they add in those balls compared with their usual level.

What counts as pressure

Leverage is how far one delivery can move the match. It climbs through the powerplay and then again at the end of a chase, and the dashed line marks the pressure cutoff.

How far to trust the ranking

Pressure results are noisy, so each estimate starts from the player’s usual level and moves toward his pressure record only as far as the evidence allows.
